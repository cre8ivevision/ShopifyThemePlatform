# Brand Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

This document defines early brand guidelines for the Shopify Theme Platform. The brand should communicate clarity, scalability, quality, and confidence for merchants, agencies, and developers.

## 2. Brand Position

The platform should be positioned as a long-term Shopify growth foundation, not a short-term visual theme.

## 3. Brand Promise

```text
Buy once. Start simple. Grow forever.
```

## 4. Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

- Clear
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Practical
- Premium
- Developer-aware
- Merchant-friendly

## 5. Messaging Pillars

| Pillar | Message |
|---|---|
| Simplicity | Launch without overwhelm |
| Growth | Expand without switching themes |
| Design | Upgrade through skins and templates |
| Performance | Speed is part of the product |
| Control | Beginner and advanced modes coexist |
| Platform | One foundation for many storefronts |

## 6. Tone of Voice

Use direct, confident language. Avoid excessive technical jargon in merchant-facing materials. Use deeper technical language in developer documentation.

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

- Brand promise is documented.
- Messaging pillars are defined.
- Tone supports both merchants and developers.
- Brand position aligns with product vision.
