# Marketplace Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines marketplace strategy for the Shopify Theme Platform. The platform should be designed for Shopify marketplace readiness while also supporting direct sales, agencies, and future extension ecosystems.

## 2. Marketplace Goals

- Meet Shopify theme quality expectations.
- Communicate platform value clearly.
- Reduce merchant confusion during purchase.
- Support premium add-ons through app or future marketplace channels.
- Build trust through documentation, demos, and support.

## 3. Positioning

The marketplace positioning should emphasise:

- Simple starting point
- Scalable growth path
- Premium skins and templates
- Performance-first architecture
- Reduced need for theme switching

## 4. Listing Requirements

Marketplace assets may include:

- Product description
- Feature list
- Demo stores
- Screenshots
- Industry examples
- Support policy
- Documentation links

## 5. Distribution Channels

Potential channels:

- Shopify Theme Store
- Direct website
- Agency partnerships
- App marketplace
- Template pack marketplace
- Future extension marketplace

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- Marketplace positioning is clear.
- Theme and app distribution paths are considered.
- Demo and documentation needs are identified.
- Future extension marketplace remains possible.
