# Business Model

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the business model for the Shopify Theme Platform. The platform should generate value through a durable theme foundation, premium design systems, companion app capabilities, and long-term merchant growth.

## 2. Business Vision

The product is not only a single Shopify theme. It is a platform that can support multiple themes, skins, templates, app features, and future marketplace extensions.

## 3. Revenue Streams

Potential revenue streams include:

- Premium theme purchase
- Companion app subscription
- Premium skins
- Template packs
- Agency licenses
- Enterprise plans
- Support and onboarding services
- Future marketplace extensions

## 4. Customer Segments

| Segment | Value Proposition |
|---|---|
| First-time merchants | Simple launch experience |
| Growing brands | Scalable theme without switching |
| Agencies | Repeatable delivery system |
| Designers | Flexible visual foundation |
| Developers | Maintainable architecture |
| Enterprise merchants | Extensible platform and governance |

## 5. Value Proposition

Core promise:

```text
Start simple. Upgrade visually. Scale without switching themes.
```

## 6. Cost Considerations

Costs may include:

- Theme development
- App development
- Support
- Documentation maintenance
- QA and testing
- Marketplace compliance
- Cloud infrastructure

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

- Business model supports theme and app revenue.
- Revenue streams align with merchant lifecycle.
- Platform positioning is clear.
- Future marketplace expansion is supported.
