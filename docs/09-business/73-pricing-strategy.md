# Pricing Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines pricing strategy principles for the Shopify Theme Platform. Pricing should reflect long-term value, merchant growth, premium design capability, and app-backed intelligence.

## 2. Pricing Principles

- Pricing should be simple to understand.
- Entry pricing should support new merchants.
- Advanced pricing should reflect increased value.
- Premium skins and templates may be packaged separately.
- App subscriptions should map to ongoing services.

## 3. Potential Pricing Layers

| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| Base Theme | One-time purchase or marketplace theme price |
| Premium Skins | Paid visual system upgrades |
| Template Packs | Industry-specific page and section packs |
| Companion App | Recurring subscription for app features |
| Agency Plan | Multi-client usage and workflow support |
| Enterprise Plan | Advanced services and governance |

## 4. Pricing Factors

Pricing may depend on:

- Feature depth
- Skin quality
- Template pack scope
- AI recommendations
- Analytics features
- Number of stores
- Support level

## 5. Packaging Strategy

The default package should feel valuable without overwhelming users. Higher tiers should unlock growth capabilities rather than basic usability.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- Pricing supports beginner and advanced merchants.
- Premium value is clearly packaged.
- Subscription pricing maps to ongoing services.
- Agency and enterprise paths are possible.
